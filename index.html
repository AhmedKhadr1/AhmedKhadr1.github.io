Ahmed Kheder

AI & Robotics Engineer in training

I'm a Computer Engineering student at Ain Shams University, building intelligent systems that learn from real-world data — from EEG biosignals to geospatial models. I work primarily in Python, with PyTorch and TensorFlow.

I enjoy moving from raw signals all the way to deployed models, and I'm especially drawn to problems at the intersection of biosignals, perception, and control. My recent work has focused on motor-imagery classification from consumer-grade EEG, and on classical ML pipelines for geospatial crime data.

Outside of class, I'm continuing to study deep learning through the DeepLearning.AI specialization and exploring how reinforcement learning could fit into the robotics problems I want to work on after graduation.

Based in Cairo, Egypt — available for opportunities.
