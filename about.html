About

A bit about me.

I'm a Computer and Systems Engineering graduate from Ain Shams University, ranked 15th of 170 in my class. I focus on LLM agents and applied machine learning.

I enjoy the whole cycle: designing the system, building it, and measuring whether it actually works. On the agent side I work with LangGraph, prompt architecture, tool design, and context and memory management. On the modelling side I use PyTorch, TensorFlow, and scikit-learn, most recently with time-series foundation models such as Chronos.

I'm comfortable moving between research ideas and clean, working code, and I care about honest evaluation — when standard metrics hide what matters, I design better ones.

What I'm most interested in right now: designing LLM agents that stay grounded, recover from their own mistakes, and accumulate useful knowledge across runs — without drifting past the spec they were given.

Based in Cairo, Egypt — open to AI/ML Engineer roles.
